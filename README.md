# Hi, I'm Charl

I'm a technologist based in Cape Town, building [Cerulean Works](https://cerulean-works.com): my independent workshop for software, machines, experiments, and field notes. I build things to explore problems and try out possible solutions, and document what I learn along the way.

I'm drawn to where technology touches the physical world, and how we interact with our tools. My current work includes software for a more intentional digital diet, writing about digital sovereignty, and the beginnings of a physical workshop.

## Currently building

- **[Cerulean Works](https://cerulean-works.com)** — the home for my projects and writing. The site is built with Astro and Markdown. [Source](https://github.com/cyberCharl/cerulean-works).
- **[Curiofold](https://github.com/cyberCharl/cerulean-crest)** — a finite, personal magazine of articles, shaped by your editorial guidelines and delivered on a schedule you choose. A connected ChatGPT curator researches and publishes editions through MCP. I started it to take more control of my own reading. [App](https://cerulean-crest.vercel.app) · [Publishing docs](https://github.com/cyberCharl/cerulean-crest/blob/main/docs/PUBLISHING.md).

Previously, I worked in Systems & Partnerships at AI Safety South Africa, facilitated AI safety courses, and hosted reading groups and hackathons.
